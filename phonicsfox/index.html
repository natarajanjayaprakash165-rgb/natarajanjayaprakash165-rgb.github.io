Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026 | Last Updated: October 28, 2026

Welcome to Phonics Fox: Learn to Read ("we", "our", or "us", application ID com.phonicsfox.app). We are dedicated to providing a safe, delightful, and private digital learning environment for young learners and families.

1. Children's Privacy & COPPA Compliance

Phonics Fox is built for early learners, preschoolers, kindergarteners, and early elementary students:

  • Zero Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, request, store, transmit, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, locations, or photos.
  • No User Account or Registration: All curriculum features are fully accessible without registration, account creation, or login credentials.
  • Parental Gate: Settings and external destinations are protected behind a math-based Parental Gate to ensure children remain safely within the learning environment.

2. Microphone Permission & Voice Processing (RECORD_AUDIO)

Phonics Fox offers optional interactive speech exercises where children practice pronouncing letter sounds and words aloud:

  • Real-Time On-Device Analysis: Voice audio is evaluated in real time strictly on the child's local device using Android's on-device speech recognizer.
  • No Audio Storage or Transmission: Audio is never recorded to persistent files, never transmitted to external servers, and never shared with third parties.
  • Explicit Activation: The microphone is engaged only when the child explicitly taps the microphone button, and immediately disengages once the prompt completes.

3. Offline Local Storage

Phonics Fox operates on a local-first offline architecture:

  • Local Storage Only: Learning milestones, earned stars, and sound progress are stored strictly on the local device using SQLite / DataStore.
  • Parental Control: Parents can reset or wipe all progress at any time through Android Settings (Settings > Apps > Phonics Fox > Storage > Clear Data) or by uninstalling the application.

4. Advertising & Google AdMob Compliance

To support free educational access, Phonics Fox integrates Google AdMob under strict Families Policy settings:

  • Families Policy Ad Filtering: All ad requests specify COPPA child-directed treatment (tagForChildDirectedTreatment = true) and under-age-of-consent parameters.
  • Non-Personalized Ads Only: Ads served are strictly contextual and family-friendly. No behavioral profiling or cross-app tracking is permitted.
  • AdMob Privacy Policy: Google's advertising practices are detailed at Google Privacy & Terms.
  • Authorized Digital Sellers: Our publisher information is publicly declared in compliance with IAB standards via app-ads.txt.
